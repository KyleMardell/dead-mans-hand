Dead Man’s Hand

Three Card Poker Roguelite

Five Western opponents stand between you and daylight. Build your poker hands, choose what to discard and earn upgrades to face the next boss.

Poker hand rankings

Strongest to weakest · Three cards only

  1. Straight FlushThree in sequence, one suit.
    J♠Q♠K♠
  2. Three of a KindThree cards of the same rank.
    K♠K♥K♣
  3. StraightThree in sequence, mixed suits.
    J♠Q♥K♣
  4. FlushOne suit, outside a sequence.
    9♠J♠A♠
  5. PairTwo cards of the same rank.
    Q♠Q♥A♠
  6. High CardNone of the combinations above.
    9♠J♥A♣

Ace is high. Suits have equal value. A–9–10 is not a straight. Within the same hand type, compare the highest relevant rank, then the remaining cards (kickers) to break a tie.

How a hand works

You and the boss each have your own independently shuffled 28-card deck: 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K and Ace in all four suits.

  1. Hold five. Look for a strong three-card hand, and think about which two cards you want to keep.
  2. Discard if you wish. Start with one discard per hand; upgrades can add more. Select cards and press Discard to draw the same number of new cards. Use your allowance together or one card at a time, while your draw pile has enough cards.
  3. Play three. Keep two. Tap a card to select it, or tap again to unselect it. Select exactly three and press Play Three against the boss’s three cards. You can play without discarding. After a discard your selection clears.
  4. Refill and continue. Your other two cards carry into the next hand; draw three to return to five. Played and discarded cards stay out for the encounter, unless the decks refresh.

First to win 3 hands takes the encounter. An exact tie awards no point; play another hand.

When the decks run low: if either side cannot refill normally, both decks refresh and both players receive five new cards. The encounter score stays.

The road through five bosses

Difficulty increases with encounter position, regardless of the character. Opponents cannot see your hidden cards or their future draws.

  1. Face four distinct opponents randomly selected from twelve regular bosses.
  2. The fifth opponent is The Undertaker, The Widow or The Preacher.

Lose one boss encounter and your run ends. Defeat all five to complete the initial run, then choose Endless to face shuffled cycles of all fifteen bosses at the fifth encounter’s difficulty. The next opponent is revealed only at their introduction.

Upgrades

After bosses 1 to 4, choose one of three randomly offered upgrades. The fifth boss completes your run; its reward is offered only if you choose Endless. They last for this run. Collect an upgrade again to improve it to Level 2; Last Stand is unique and can only be taken once.

Maxed upgrades and an owned Last Stand are not offered again. If fewer than three eligible upgrades remain, you see fewer choices; when none remain, you continue without a reward. Starting a new run clears your upgrades.

Quick Draw
Two discards per hand at Level 1; three at Level 2.
Lucky Break
Discard one selected card and choose between two drawn cards; the other goes out. Costs one normal discard. One use per boss, or two at Level 2.
Flexible Carry
Between continuing hands, keep two and draw four, then choose one of the six to discard. Costs no normal discard. One use per boss, or two at Level 2; requires enough draw cards.
Read the Table
Once per boss, reveal the rank of one card from the boss’s already committed trio; two distinct cards at Level 2. Suits stay hidden. Costs no discard.
Last Stand
Starting with the next hand after the boss is one win from taking the encounter, gain two extra discards per hand for that encounter. Adds to Quick Draw.
Peek Ahead
Always see your actual next draw’s rank; Level 2 adds its suit. Costs nothing and never changes deck order.

Active ability uses reset for each boss, not each hand or deck refresh. Open Upgrades at the table to review your collection.
