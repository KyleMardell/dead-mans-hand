The rules of the table

Each side has its own 24-card deck: 9 through Ace, four suits. Draw five, optionally replace one card, then choose three to play. Your other two cards carry into the next hand; draw back to five.

First to two wins takes the encounter. Equal hands score nothing. Lose one encounter and the run ends. Beat five bosses to finish, then choose endless if you wish.

  1. Straight flush
  2. Three of a kind
  3. Straight
  4. Flush
  5. Pair
  6. High card

Higher ranks break ties, then kickers. Suits are equal. Ace is high; A–9–10 does not form a straight. Played and discarded cards stay out of your deck until the next boss.

Approved tie exception: if either side cannot refill to five, both receive fresh decks and five new cards. The score stays. The table announces this.

Bosses follow your rules, with different decision skills. They cannot see your hidden cards or their next draw. No shop, upgrades or extra lives.

NO WAGERS. NO SECOND LIVES. JUST THE CARDS YOU KEEP.
